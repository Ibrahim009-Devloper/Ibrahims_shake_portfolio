    ⚡ What It Does

    • Automated Price Tracking: Automatically extracts competitor product pricing and stock across multi-domain target stores.
    • Delta & Stock Detection: Identifies price increases, markdowns, and back-in-stock events without manual checks.
    • Real-Time Telegram Alerts: Dispatches instant alert webhooks with structured product details and direct links.
    • Historical Trends: Persists all snapshots for trend analytics and competitor discount patterns.

    🔄 How It Works

    • 1. Target Queue: Reads monitored SKU URLs from configuration or persistent store.
    • 2. Headless Collection: Playwright navigates target pages, executes dynamic scripts, and grabs clean JSON payloads.
    • 3. Diff Analysis: Compares observed prices against previous records in SQLite / PostgreSQL.
    • 4. Instant Dispatch: Fires structured Telegram notifications with automated error handling and retry resilience.

    💼 Business Benefits

    • Eliminates 6 to 8 hours of tedious daily manual checks across competitor catalogs.
    • Immediate awareness of competitor promotions and flash sales within seconds.
    • Reliable autonomous runs without human intervention, equipped with auto-recovery health checks.

    🛠️ Tech Stack & Infrastructure

    • Core Language: Python 3.11+
    • Browser Automation: Playwright (Headless Chromium)
    • Data Persistence: SQLite & PostgreSQL
    • Delivery: Telegram Bot API (Async Webhooks)
    • CI/CD & Testing: GitHub Actions, Pytest
